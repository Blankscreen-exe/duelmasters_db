Fire Nature Dreammate Swarm

40 cards12 unique Nature Fire
Strategy & notes

Strategy: Go-wide aggro. Flood the board with cheap Dreammates and Vehicle Bees (Kabuto 1, Ralbin, Pakoneko, Rinpao) on turns 2-3. Jangaljan makes every Nature creature cost 1 less and Peach Princess makes your second creature each turn cost up to 2 less, so turns 3-5 often drop 2-3 creatures. Hopstep Battan is a 4-cost 7000 Double Breaker that survives as long as you keep playing a Dreammate or Vehicle Bee each turn. Homelos evolves onto any Dreammate and attacks immediately as a 9000 Double Breaker. Finish with Daigoku Ogre (all Dreammates and Vehicle Bees get Double Breaker) or a Lunatic Energy (+4000 to all your creatures) alpha strike. Comet Missile clears blockers.

Notes: User request: fast attacker deck that spawns monsters rapidly and overwhelms with numbers, Fire and Nature only. Weaknesses: board wipes that hit small creatures (e.g. 'destroy all creatures with power 2000 or less'), lots of blockers (only 2 Comet Missile), no card draw so it can run out of cards in long games. Swap options: Step Step Antikon (Fire 3, Vehicle Bee, 4000 with a Dreammate), Megamusashi Dragonflyer (Fire 5, Dreammates/Vehicle Bees get power attacker +3000), Elf-X (Nature 4, all your creatures cost 1 less), Sergeant Stag Beetler (more blocker removal).
