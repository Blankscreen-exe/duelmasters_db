Fire Nature Evolution Beatdown

46 cards16 unique Fire Nature
Strategy & notes

Strategy: Evolution beatdown. Turns 2-3: cheap Human and Beast Folk bases (Gonta counts as both) plus Nature ramp (Faerie Life, Bronze-Arm Tribe). Evolve the bases into power monsters that attack immediately: Barkwhip (2 cost, 5000), Armored Blaster Valdios (Double Breaker, Humans +1000), Armored Decimator Valkaizer (destroys a creature with 4000 power or less), Fighter Dual Fang (8000 Double Breaker, ramps 2). Ramp pays for Explosive Fighter Ucarn's 9000 Double Breaker and its 2-mana drawback. Searing Wave wipes the opponent's small creatures and blockers before a big attack; Tornado Flame is removal and a Shield Trigger; Magma Gazer turns any attacker into a Double Breaker for the finishing push. Extras (46-card version): Peacock Breath gives every creature power attacker +6000 while you have an evolution out, turning even 2-cost bases into Double Breakers for a finishing swing; Power Blazer is an extra Beast Folk base that becomes 5000 power with an evolution out; Crimson Hammer clears small blockers early.

Notes: Built from the user's direction: Human evolutions, Ucarn, Beast Folk evolutions (Barkwhip, Fighter Dual Fang), Searing Wave, destroyer and power attacker spells, Nature for mana. Weaknesses: no card draw; only 6 Shield Triggers; Searing Wave costs one of your own shields; evolutions are dead cards without a base. Expanded from 40 to 46 cards (house rule allows 40-50) with Crimson Hammer x2, Peacock Breath x2, Power Blazer x2. Further options: Earth Ripper (Beast Folk evolution Double Breaker).
