Armored Dragon Overlords

40 cards15 unique Fire Nature
Strategy & notes

Strategy: Fire/Nature Armored Dragon ramp. Turns 2-3: Faerie Life, Bronze-Arm Tribe and Cocco Lupia (Dragons cost 2 less, minimum 2). From turn 4 drop 6-cost Armored Dragons, then evolve them into Überdragons. Bolshack NEX pulls a Cocco Lupia from the deck straight into the battle zone. Velyrika Dragon tutors more Armored Dragons. Valkerios Dragon is free once 2+ Armored Dragons are in play. Überdragon Bash Mikazuchi costs 3 with Cocco and makes the opponent destroy one of their own creatures whenever they attack. Überdragon Valkyrias cheats a Dragon from mana into play. Überdragon Bajula triple-breaks and destroys mana. Totto Pipicchi gives every Dragon speed attacker. Removal: Volcanic Arrows, Natural Snare, Bolmeteus Musha Dragon and Dolzark (whenever another Dragon attacks, sends a creature with 5000 or less power to mana). Mulligan for ramp or Cocco Lupia. Putting a 6-drop Dragon in mana early is fine because Valkyrias can bring it back.

Notes: The user asked for a tribal Dragon deck built around Armored Dragons and dragon evolutions, any civilizations and sets. Weaknesses: no blockers or card draw; slow against fast rush if the triggers don't show up; Volcanic Arrows and Musha Dragon cost your own shields. Swap options: Spiritual Star Dragon (tutors a Dragon evolution), Überdragon Jabaha, Bolsred Fire Dragon, Lupia Lapia, Evolution Egg.
