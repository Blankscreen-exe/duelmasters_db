FIRE VALDIOS ASSAULT  (43 cards)
============================================================
Civilizations: Fire 34 / Nature 16 cards   Average cost: 3.44
Deck rule: 40-50 cards (40 ideal), max 4 copies per card

FIRE HUMAN BASES
----------------
4x Brawler Zyler  [Fire, cost 2]
     Cheap Human, attacks at 3000
4x Gonta, the Warrior Savage  [Fire/Nature, cost 2]
     4000 power; counts as Human (for Valdios) and Beast Folk (for Barkwhip and Dual Fang)
3x Explosive Dude Joe  [Fire, cost 3]
     Solid 3000-power Human base
3x Brad, Super Kickin' Dynamo  [Fire, cost 3]
     Human; destroys a blocker each turn he starts tapped (after he has attacked)
3x Wind Axe, the Warrior Savage  [Fire/Nature, cost 5]
     Human and Beast Folk; destroys a blocker and adds 1 mana when it comes in

POWER MONSTERS
--------------
2x Divine Machine Armor Valborg  [Fire, cost 3]
     Human evolution; Double Breaker for 3, +1000 power per other Fire creature when attacking
4x Armored Blaster Valdios  [Fire, cost 4]
     MAIN BOSS: Double Breaker; your other Humans get +1000
2x Armored Decimator Valkaizer  [Fire, cost 5]
     Human evolution; destroys a creature with 4000 power or less
3x Explosive Fighter Ucarn  [Fire, cost 5]
     9000 power, Double Breaker (you put 2 mana cards into your graveyard)
2x Barkwhip, the Smasher  [Nature, cost 2]
     Beast Folk evolution, 5000 power; goes on Gonta or Wind Axe
3x Fighter Dual Fang  [Nature, cost 6]
     Beast Folk evolution, 8000 power, Double Breaker, adds 2 mana

SPELLS
------
4x Faerie Life  [Nature, cost 2]
     Shield Trigger; adds 1 mana
2x The Strong Crush  [Fire, cost 2]
     Destroys a blocker; draws a card if you have a 6000+ power creature
2x Peacock Breath  [Fire, cost 4]
     With an evolution out, all your creatures get Power attacker +6000; 6000+ attackers break 2 shields
2x Searing Wave  [Fire, cost 5]
     Destroys all opponent creatures with 3000 power or less (costs one of your shields)

GAME PLAN
---------
- Turns 2-3: play Human bases and Faerie Life for extra mana.
- Turns 3-4: evolve into Valborg (3-cost Double Breaker) or Valdios (Double Breaker, other Humans +1000).
- Clear blockers with Brad, Wind Axe and The Strong Crush; Valkaizer and Searing Wave clear small attackers.
- Nature only ramps and supplies Barkwhip and Fighter Dual Fang, which go on Gonta or Wind Axe.
- Heavy hitters: Ucarn and Fighter Dual Fang. Finish with Peacock Breath to turn the whole board into Double Breakers.

DECK STATS
----------
Mana curve: 2-cost: 16, 3-cost: 8, 4-cost: 6, 5-cost: 10, 6-cost: 3
Double Breakers or better: 12   Removal: 6   Ramp: 10   Card draw: 2   Shield Triggers: 4

WEAKNESSES
----------
- Only 7 bases for the Beast Folk evolutions (Gonta x4, Wind Axe x3).
- Only 4 Shield Triggers.
- Searing Wave costs you a shield; Brad has to attack before his ability works.

SWAP OPTIONS
------------
- Bronze-Arm Tribe: Nature ramp creature and Beast Folk base (more bases for Barkwhip/Dual Fang)
- Comet Missile / Tornado Flame: Fire Shield Triggers with removal
- Shock Trooper Mykee: Human Speed Attacker that destroys a creature
- Armored Raider Gandaval: Human evolution Double Breaker

Card strength notes are judgment, not tested in games.
